## The Collection

1. **What do you call a twentieth-order cumulant?**  
   You don't. Your grandchild does.

2. **What do you call a theory that crumbles under multipath?**  
   Aimless.

3. **What did the mixer do when it saw the LDO run away?**  
   It broke down.

4. **Why did the garbage truck take away the manifold?**  
   It was up for compaction.

5. **Why did the category theorist go bankrupt?**  
   Self-explanatory.

6. **Why was the cat afraid of the topologist?**  
   He had bread for breakfast.
